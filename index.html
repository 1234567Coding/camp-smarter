Start here

Camp Smarter is for first- and second-year car campers who are overwhelmed by gear lists and jargon. Our promise: no gatekeeping, no 47-item packing lists, and no pretending a $400 gadget is "essential." Every guide answers three questions — what actually matters, what it costs, and what you can skip.

We're budget-conscious and safety-first. That means we never shame a $70 tent that keeps you dry, we call out overpriced hype when the cheap option does the same job, and we treat safety rules (fire, weather, wildlife, Leave No Trace) as non-negotiable — not as a lecture, but as the reason you get to come back next weekend.
